This system is based on the following entity relationships:

  • Patient - has attributes: name, PID, sex, address, contact_no, Insurance_Info
  • Doctor - has attributes: D_name, specialty, Phys_ID
  • Drug - has attributes: Trade_Name, Price, Quantity
  • Drug_Manufacturer - has attributes: Name, Company_ID, Address
  • Employee - has attributes: Name, Employee_ID, Shift_Start, Shift_End
  • Pharmacy - has attributes: Pharm_ID, name, Address, Fax

Key relationships include:

  • Patients are Seen By Doctors
  • Doctors Prescribe Drugs
  • Drug Manufacturers Make Drugs
  • Employees Sell Drugs
  • Employees Work at Pharmacies
  • Drug Manufacturers have Contracts with Pharmacies
